{% trans "About Us" %}

Kisaan {% blocktrans %}is a simple and useful web application that directly connects Nepali farmers and customers. Now, farmers can easily upload their vegetables, fruits, or other agricultural products online through mobile or computer. Customers can also view and purchase fresh products directly from nearby farmers.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Our app provides a special dashboard for farmers where they can post their products, check monthly or yearly income, and directly communicate with customers.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}We want farmers to get a fair price, customers to get fresh products, and trust to be built between both sides. The app also allows location-based search, so customers can choose nearby farmers.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Through technology, we aim to reduce the distance between villages and cities, make agricultural trade easier, and help increase farmers’ income.{% endblocktrans %}
